## Wrinkle relaxers (Botox, Dysport)
Neuromodulators that temporarily relax the facial muscles that cause dynamic wrinkles: forehead lines, crow's feet, and frown lines between the brows. The site says they require no downtime and smoothing is visible within days. Pricing: Botox starts at $13/unit; Dysport starts at $5/unit (before discounts or rewards).

## Collagen stimulators (Sculptra, hyperdiluted Radiesse)
Biostimulators that trigger natural collagen production over time to improve firmness, texture and volume. Results develop over weeks to months. Both start at $800.

## Dermal fillers (hyaluronic acid)
Restore volume, smooth lines, enhance contours and add lip definition. Products listed: Restylane family (including Restylane Contour and Restylane Eyelight), Belotero, Radiesse; Juvederm family and Voluma XC by special order. Fillers start from $600.

## Fat-dissolving injection (deoxycholic acid)
Non-surgical treatment for fat under the chin. Most patients need multiple sessions. Starts at $250 per vial.

## Filler reversal (Hylenex)
An enzyme that dissolves hyaluronic acid filler to address overfilled areas, lumps or asymmetry, or to reverse previous filler. Starts at $250 per vial; ultrasound-guided dissolving costs extra for patients within 3 months of injection or with palpable filler.

## PRP facial injections and PRP Selphyller
Platelet-rich plasma from the patient's own blood, injected to support collagen and improve texture, tone and elasticity (under-eyes, cheeks, nasolabial folds, jawline, fine lines). Selphyller uses a platelet-rich fibrin gel to restore subtle volume. Pricing not listed.

## What an injectable appointment is like
Most injectables contain lidocaine and topical numbing cream is available. Appointments typically take 15–45 minutes depending on the number of areas. The site says patients might notice some immediate swelling or minor bruising and most feel comfortable returning to normal activities right away. Detailed aftercare is given by the provider.
