## Weight management program
A medically managed program. Medications offered include semaglutide (Ozempic®, Wegovy®) and tirzepatide (Zepbound®, Mounjaro®), in commercial and compounded forms, plus Lipo-Mino injections (B vitamins and amino acids) and CaloCurb. Retatrutide is not available (still in clinical trials). The process: book a consultation, the team learns your goals and history, labs assess hormones, metabolism, thyroid and insulin sensitivity, then a personalized plan that may include nutrition and lifestyle guidance. No membership required; pricing is discussed at consultation. Medication choice and dosing are decided only by the clinic's providers.
