## Location and contact
Essence Aesthetics & Wellness is a med spa at 1840 Lakeview Dr., Suite 200, Indianapolis, IN 46250. Phone: (317) 555-0142. Email: care@essence-wellness.example. Clinic hours are not listed on the website; patients should call or check the booking page.

## Booking and payment
Appointments and consultations are booked online: https://www.essence-wellness.example/book . The clinic accepts CareCredit. The clinic describes a "zero-pressure" model where patients are educated to choose their own care plan. Consultations are described as pressure-free with any provider.
