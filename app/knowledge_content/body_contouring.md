## Body contouring options
Essence lists these body contouring treatments: truSculpt iD (non-invasive radiofrequency to reduce fat and tighten skin on the abdomen, flanks, thighs, arms, back, jowls and under the chin; results develop gradually over about 12 weeks; from $600 per cycle, promotional), InMode Ignite RF (non-surgical radiofrequency body contouring, anesthetic options available; from $2,500, promotional), RF Microneedling (from $400 on this page), Microneedling (from $250), PRXT 33 Italian Body Peel (five sessions about ten days apart; from $350), European threadlifting (priced by area) and Sculptra / hyperdiluted Radiesse for body areas (from $800).

## CoolSculpting
CoolSculpting is not listed on the Essence website. The clinic's listed non-invasive fat-reduction options are truSculpt iD and InMode Ignite RF; a consultation can help decide what fits.
